## Core thesis

The current AI cycle should be analyzed as an infrastructure rebuild, not as a generic software theme.

The most important principle:

> Do not buy “AI concepts.” Research the bottlenecks locked into real procurement chains from NVIDIA, Google, OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft, Oracle, and CoreWeave.

The working framework:

```text
Order hardness
× Profit retention
× Capacity bottleneck
× Technology roadmap position
× Valuation and risk
```

## Evidence discipline

Never treat rumors as confirmed orders.

Evidence levels:

- **S**: official company announcement, exchange filing, SEC filing, or both sides publicly confirm.
- **A**: Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, TrendForce, LightCounting, Omdia, or other authoritative industry sources.
- **B**: sell-side research, company-only statements, investor relations notes, strong industry inference.
- **C**: social media, unsourced market rumors, unverified investor forums.

## Important caveats

- Revenue growth is not the same as profit growth.
- AI cloud contracts can be huge but cash-flow negative.
- Data center projects face power, permitting, transformer, cooling, and financing bottlenecks.
- A company related to a technology route is not necessarily related to NVIDIA or Google orders.
- Technical migration can destroy old winners and create new winners.

## Usage guideline

For every future report:

1. Verify new facts from primary or authoritative sources.
2. Classify evidence level.
3. Update order hardness.
4. Check gross margin, net margin, cash flow, receivables, and inventory.
5. Identify technology roadmap changes.
6. Reclassify companies between core, growth, high-upside, and observation buckets.
